# Hindi With Kishan — Starter Website

## Files
- `index.html` — page content and layout
- `style.css` — colors, responsive layout and styling
- `script.js` — mobile menu and demo enquiry form

## Open in VS Code
1. Extract the ZIP file to a folder.
2. Open VS Code.
3. Choose **File → Open Folder** and select `hindi_with_kishan_website`.
4. Open `index.html`.
5. If you have the **Live Server** extension, right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**. Otherwise, double-click `index.html` to preview it in your browser.

## Before publishing
1. In `script.js`, replace `YOUR_EMAIL@example.com` with your real teaching email.
2. Update the About section so it accurately reflects your teaching background and experience.
3. Confirm the fees, lesson length and availability before adding them to the website.
4. The enquiry form uses the visitor's email app (`mailto:`). It does not store enquiries or send them to a server. For a live site, connect a form service or secure backend.
5. Use only photos and testimonials you have permission to publish. Obtain parent/guardian consent for any child's photo or information.
6. The Google Fonts link requires internet access; system fonts will be used if it cannot load.


FREE TRIAL BOOKING FORM
The form collects parent name, email, country/time zone, child age and Hindi level, preferred day/time, and learning goals. It opens the visitor's email app and prepares a request for a free Google Meet trial lesson. It does not automatically store submissions, confirm appointments, or create Google Meet links.
Before publishing, open script.js and replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your real teaching email address.


PAYMENT OPTION
- The payment section displays the provided UPI QR image and UPI ID: baghot@okaxis.
- The introductory trial is free. Tell parents to confirm the fee before making any payment.
- The website includes a UPI-app link and a Copy UPI ID button. Browser/device support for UPI links varies.
- Do not publish a bank passbook image or full bank account details on a public website. Share bank-transfer details privately only when needed.

BOOKING FORM
- Booking requests open the visitor's email app and are addressed to bagoht@gmail.com.
- This static site does not store bookings in a database and does not automatically send Google Meet links.
- Test the form and payment QR on your own devices before publishing.
