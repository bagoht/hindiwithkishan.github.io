MY TEACHING METHOD

Hindi made simple, one little step at a time.

Children do not need to memorize long lists. They need to hear, try, play and use new words in a friendly space.

🗣️
STEP 01

Speak from day one

We start with useful everyday phrases, greetings and simple questions. Children practise speaking without fear of making mistakes.

नमस्ते!Namaste! — Hello!
🧩
STEP 02

Learn through play

Pictures, colours, objects, matching games and fun activities help new words stick naturally.

सेब 🍎Seb — Apple
📖
STEP 03

Build reading skills

When a child is ready, we introduce Devanagari letters, sounds, easy words and short sentences at a comfortable pace.

क · ख · गKa · Kha · Ga
🌏
STEP 04

Connect with culture

Stories, family words, celebrations and everyday Indian life make language meaningful and personal.

धन्यवादDhanyavaad — Thank you
A TINY TASTE OF A LESSON

Learn a useful phrase today!

We connect a Hindi phrase with its meaning and practise it in a real-life situation.

The goal is not perfect Hindi on day one. The goal is to feel comfortable trying.

YOUR FIRST PHRASE🌟
मेरा नाम ___ है।
Mera naam ___ hai.
My name is ___.
Try saying it with your own name!

A LITTLE ABOUT YOUR TUTOR

Meet Kishan

Hi! I’m Kishan, a friendly and passionate Hindi tutor from India who loves making language learning simple, enjoyable, and meaningful.

I teach Hindi online to children aged 4–15, especially children growing up in NRI families who want to stay connected to the Hindi language and Indian culture.

My lessons are designed around each child’s age, interests, and learning level. I use both English and Hindi to explain new words, practise everyday conversations, and make learning fun through pictures, stories, games, and real-life examples.

My goal is not just to help children learn Hindi words, but to help them understand the language, speak with confidence, and enjoy learning at their own pace.

Every child learns differently, so I believe in patience, encouragement, and personalized lessons. Let’s make learning Hindi a happy and rewarding experience for your child!
