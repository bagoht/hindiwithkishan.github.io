Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Hindi With Kishan respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit this website or request an online Hindi lesson.

Information you choose to provide

If you submit the free-trial form, it opens your own email application with the information you entered. This static website does not itself store or submit those form details to a database. Your email provider will handle the message under its own privacy terms.

Please do not send sensitive personal information about a child. A parent or legal guardian should make lesson enquiries and provide only the information reasonably needed to arrange a lesson.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may use third-party services in the future, such as Google AdSense, Google Analytics, embedded YouTube videos or similar tools. Those services may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as browser details, IP address and page activity according to their own policies. If advertising or analytics are enabled, this policy should be updated to describe the services actually in use.

Google explains how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services here: How Google uses information.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended to help parents and guardians arrange lessons for children. Parents or guardians should complete enquiry forms and communicate with the tutor. Do not knowingly submit unnecessary personal information about a child.

Third-party links

The website may link to services such as Google Meet, YouTube or email providers. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, email bagoht@gmail.com.

Before publishing, review this policy and update it to match the services and tracking tools you actually use.
